Precept Recitation days

Which days carry the Precept Recitation flag (布薩, bùsà), in every view — the days the assembly gathers to recite the precepts. Traditionally two a month, at the full moon and at the month's end.

Where this is kept

These settings, and the filters chosen on the record, are saved in this browser only, under the key bodhi.settings. Nothing is sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data resets them.

Lunar dates come from the Hong Kong Observatory conversion tables. Dharma events and everything up to 31 January 2027 come from the DRBA printed calendar for 2026.

The six fast days are the 8th, 14th, 15th, 23rd and the last two days of each lunar month, following 佛說四天王經 (Fo shuo si tianwang jing, Taisho T15n0590). The long fasting months are the 1st, 5th and 9th. New and full moon follow the calendar convention, day 1 and day 15, not the astronomical instant.

A leap month is counted as a long fasting month here and flagged wherever it appears. That reading is provisional and still to be confirmed.
